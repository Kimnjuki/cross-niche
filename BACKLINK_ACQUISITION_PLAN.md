# Backlink Acquisition Plan — The Grid Nexus

Free, high-value backlinks are earned, not created — no tool can conjure them
without the site owner's accounts. This plan prioritizes the two assets most
likely to attract them: the **free Gaming Security Checkup tool**
(`/tools/gaming-security-checkup`) and the **original benchmark data** (antivirus
FPS benchmarks, VPN latency tests, Steam takeover statistics).

## The two linkable assets

1. **Gaming Security Checkup** — a free, in-browser tool that stores nothing.
   Free tools are the #1 linkable asset; every tool directory and "best tools"
   listicle links to them.
2. **Original data reports** — `/article/gaming-pc-antivirus-best-2026`
   (six products benchmarked), `/article/vpn-gaming-security-latency-test-2026`
   (eight VPNs, twelve games), `/article/steam-account-takeover-protection-guide-2026`
   (340% surge stat). Journalists and roundups cite original numbers.

## Medium-leverage (one-time effort)

- **Wikipedia/Wikidata** — once the site has third-party coverage, add a
  Wikidata entity (notable org) so the brand resolves as a knowledge-graph node.
- **"Best tools" outreach** — email 10 roundup authors (search
  `"best gaming security tools"` + "guest post" / "submit your tool") offering
  the free checker. Roundups are the highest-ROI one-to-many link source.
- **Digital PR** — pitch the Steam 340% stat and the antivirus FPS benchmark to
  gaming/security journalists; a single citation from a gaming outlet compounds.

## Rules

- Do NOT buy links or join link farms — a manual penalty costs more than any gain.
- Prioritize dofollow tool directories + original-data citations over low-DR
  directory spam.
- Every link should come from a page that's genuinely useful to that site's
  audience, or it won't stick.
