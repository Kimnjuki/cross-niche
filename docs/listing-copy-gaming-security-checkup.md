## AlternativeTo

**Name:** Gaming Security Checkup

**Tagline (one line):**
Free 7-point security audit for Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, Riot, Epic & Battle.net — get your Fort-Knox-to-Exposed tier in 3 minutes.

**Description (short):**
Run a platform-specific account security audit across six gaming platforms. Each platform gets a tailored, weighted 7-point checklist covering MFA/2FA, password reuse, privacy settings, API keys, login history and payment exposure — then you get a scored tier (Fort Knox → Exposed) with exact fix steps for every gap. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is stored or uploaded.

**Features (bullets):**
- Platform-specific checklists for Steam, PlayStation Network, Xbox/Microsoft, Riot Games, Epic Games, and Battle.net
- Weighted scoring — enabling mobile 2FA is worth more than a privacy toggle, so your score reflects real risk
- Four security tiers: Fort Knox (90–100%), Secured (70–89%), Hardened (50–69%), Exposed (0–49%)
- Every failed check ships a "how to check" path and a direct link to the right settings page
- Critical-fix prioritization — high-impact gaps (2FA, password reuse) are surfaced first
- 100% client-side: no account, no sign-up, nothing stored or uploaded

## Product Hunt

**Name:** Gaming Security Checkup

**Tagline:**
Get your gaming accounts' security tier in 3 minutes — free, nothing stored.

**Description (the "what & why"):**
Your Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, Riot, Epic and Battle.net accounts are worth more than you think — rare skins, V-Bucks, WoW items and stored payment methods. Gaming account takeovers are up 340% in 2026.

Gaming Security Checkup gives you a platform-specific, weighted 7-point audit for each account: is mobile 2FA on? Is your password reused? Are your profile and playtime public? Any rogue API keys or unfamiliar logins?

You get a scored tier — Fort Knox, Secured, Hardened or Exposed — and, for every gap, the exact menu path and a direct link to fix it. No account. No sign-up. Nothing stored — it runs entirely in your browser.

**Topics:** Security, Gaming, Privacy, Developer Tools

**Maker's first comment:**
Hi PH — I'm the founder of The Grid Nexus, a gaming & security intelligence platform.

The idea came from our reporting on the 340% surge in Steam account takeovers this year. The #1 driver is credential stuffing, and the #1 fix is a few account settings — but the settings are buried in six different platform menus. So we built a 3-minute checkup that tells you exactly which toggles to flip, in the order that matters.

A few choices worth noting:
- Scores are weighted. Enabling mobile 2FA (25 pts) moves your score more than hiding your real name (5 pts), because it protects you more.
- It's genuinely private. There's no login wall and nothing is uploaded — the audit runs client-side, and we only log an anonymous score band to see if the tool is helping.
- Every fix links straight to the right settings page (Steam Guard, account.microsoft.com, auth.riotgames.com, etc.), so you're not Googling menu paths.

Would love feedback on which platform to add next (Nintendo? Ubisoft?) and whether an exportable report would be useful.
