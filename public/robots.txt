# ── AI & search policy ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# We WANT to be found by search engines AND every AI assistant (Google AI
# Overviews, Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, Apple Intelligence).
# AI answer citations are a primary referral channel, so all crawlers — search,
# AI search/retrieval (ai-input), and AI training (ai-train) — are allowed.
# We publish llms.txt and llms-full.txt precisely so AI systems can read us.

Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes, use=full
